The Tenth of September, MMXXVI
Dear Hanim,

May the skies above you always remain gentle, and may the sea carry away every sorrow that finds its way to your shore.

I hope life is kind to you, in the quiet moments and in the days that ask a little more of you. May you find reasons to smile in the smallest things, and may there always be someone beside you when the world feels a little too vast.

I am grateful, in ways I seldom know how to say, that somewhere along this long road of life, our paths happened to cross. There are people who merely pass through our days, and there are those who, without trying, make the days feel a little brighter.

You, somehow, are the latter.

So here is to another year beneath the same moon, another chapter waiting to be written, and countless sunsets yet to see.

May the world be gentle with you, may your heart remain light, and may you always know that your presence has made this little corner of the world a much warmer place.

Happy Birthday. I hope this year brings you everything beautiful that you have quietly wished for. 🌙

With all my love & warmest wishes,
